📖 Manual — How This Tracker Works

A quick walkthrough of every section, so nothing feels like a mystery button.

📲 Home Screen Shortcuts

Once installed (Add to Home Screen / APK), long-press the app icon for quick actions: Mark Present, Daily Qs, Battle Log, and Data & Backup — each jumps straight to that screen (or logs your present day) without opening the app fully first.

👑 HUD (top of page)

  • Level / XP bar Your overall level, driven by checklist completion, tests logged, and streak.
  • Streak Consecutive days you've marked yourself present. Missing a day resets it.
  • Mark Present Tap once per day to log attendance and keep your streak alive.
  • Battle Tag A name you set so you can appear on the shared Leaderboard.

📊 Subject Completion Cards

  • The cards under the HUD show each subject's checklist % and chapters completed.

🎬 Stories Tab

A themed recap card for each subject (and Advanced), plus your all-time question count for it, pulled from the Daily Qs log. The same count appears as a small badge on each subject's nav button.

⚖️ Subject Balance Nudge

Appears automatically if one subject is far ahead of another (25%+ gap in checklist completion). It's a nudge to stop over-preparing one subject while neglecting another — a common cause of lopsided scores.

💾 Data Tab — Auto-Save & ⬇ Backup / ⬆ Restore / 🖨 Weekly Report / 🗑 Reset

  • Auto-Save Every checklist tick, test entry, and daily log is saved automatically to this device's local storage the instant you make it — nothing to remember, no sync step, and it works fully offline.
  • Download Backup Optional — exports everything (checklists, tests, streak, daily questions) as a .json file, useful if you're moving to a new phone or want an extra copy.
  • Restore Backup Loads a previously downloaded .json backup, replacing current data on this device.
  • Weekly Report Opens right inside the app in a preview panel — a printable summary of the last 7 days: checklist progress, no. of questions practiced (per subject + total), tests logged, streak, and present days. Use the panel's Print button (or Download to save the .html file first) to share it with a parent or mentor.
  • Reset All Data Wipes everything on this device — checklists, tests, streak, daily log. Opens a confirmation popup where you must type RESET before it will proceed, since it can't be undone. Download a backup first if you might want the data later.

🔊 Sound Effects

Short chimes play for ticking a checkbox, completing a chapter, logging a test or daily questions, marking yourself present, leveling up, and deleting entries. They're generated on-device (no audio files), so they work fully offline. Toggle them on/off anytime from the Data tab — your choice is remembered.

↩ Undo

Deleting a chapter test, mock test, or daily question shows an "Undo" toast at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds — tap it to instantly restore what you just removed.

🔁 Revision Due

In the Booyah Badges tab, alongside the Weak Spot Radar. Once a chapter's checklist first hits 90%+, its completion date is remembered. If 20+ days pass with no chapter test logged for it since, it shows up here — a nudge to revisit chapters you've marked done rather than only touching them once.

🔁 Revision Counter (per chapter)

Every Physics/Chemistry/Maths chapter card has a small "Revisions" row with − and + buttons and a count in between. Tap + each time you go back and revise that chapter — it's a manual tally you control, separate from the checklist and from Revision Due, just to track how many passes you've made over a topic.

📅 Study Reminders (70% chapters)

The moment a chapter's checklist first crosses 70%, its date is remembered. From there: day 2 and day 5 show up here nudging you to revise your notes, and day 8+ switches to nudging you to actually take a test on it. Shows in the Booyah Badges tab below Revision Due, and if Streak Reminders are turned on in the Data tab, it also sends one evening notification a day when something's due.

⬆️⬇️ Chapter priority emojis

Each chapter shows a small arrow next to its name: ⬆️ = high-yield (heavily weighted in JEE Main PYQs 2019-2026), no arrow = moderate, ⬇️ = lower-yield. This comes from real chapter-wise and year-wise PYQ breakdowns plus a high-yield priority list — not a guess. Physics, Chemistry and Maths all have full year-by-year detail now. Two chapters (Physics Practical Skills, Chemistry Purification & Characterisation) have no matching data at all and simply show no arrow.

📊 Chapter Analysis (own tab)

A dedicated tab — pick Physics, Chemistry, or Maths at the top (Physics shows by default) for a full chapter-by-chapter analysis: priority arrow, difficulty tag, estimated PYQ pool, weightage %, easy/medium/hard split, and a one-line strategic action plan per chapter, sorted highest-priority first. Below the chapter cards sits a weightage bar chart and a pie chart of chapter weightage, and a year-wise question distribution chart (all three subjects now have this) with a dropdown to isolate a single chapter's trend.

📅 Per-Chapter Year View

On any chapter card in the Mathematics tab where year-wise data exists, a small 📅 button sits next to the chapter title. Tap it to expand a mini bar chart right there showing how many questions that specific chapter contributed each year from 2019 to 2026 — no need to leave the checklist to see it.

⏱ Time Tracking (Daily Qs)

The Daily Qs form has an optional "Time Taken (min)" field. Once you fill it alongside question count, the day's summary and the Weekly Report both show your average pace (seconds per question) — useful for gauging exam-day speed, not just accuracy.

🏷 Mistake Type tagging

The Chapter Skirmish Log has optional Mistake Type chips (Silly Mistake / Concept Gap / Time Pressure / Guessed Wrong / None) — tap as many as apply to one test, not just one. The Weak Spot Radar shows your most common mistake type per weak chapter — so you know not just what's weak, but why.

🗓 Exam Countdown

Set your JEE Main/Advanced date in the Data tab — a day counter then shows right under the HUD on every page. Clear it anytime from the same place.

📊 Mock Analysis (Final Zone Log)

The Final Zone mock form is laid out as one row per subject — Physics / Chemistry / Maths, each with just Attempted (out of 25) and Correct. You don't enter or add up any scores: total score and negative marks are calculated automatically (standard +4 per correct, −1 per wrong MCQ, 25 Qs per subject). There's also an optional "Topics Covered" field, comma-separated, shown in the table and printable report instead of relying on the test name alone.

The "Mock Analysis" panel below the trend chart shows all-time average section scores, plus — for whichever mock you're viewing — a segmented bar per subject: green (correct, with the count inside), red (wrong), and grey (unattempted), all out of 25. Tap any row in the Final Zone Log table to switch the analysis to that specific mock, or use the ← Older / Newer → controls below the bars.

🖨 Printable Mock Analysis (All-Time)

A button right below the Mock Analysis panel opens a full printable report covering every Final Zone mock you've ever logged — average score, best/weakest mock, an improving/declining trend, section-wise averages, negative-marking analysis, and now a dedicated "Analysis of Every Test" section that gives each individual mock its own breakdown (score, negative marks, section scores, correct/wrong/unattempted) rather than just one summary row. Opens in the same in-app preview panel as the Weekly Report, with Print and Download buttons.

🖨 Print This Mock

Next to the all-time report button is a second one — "Print This Mock" — which prints only whichever single mock you're currently viewing in the Mock Analysis panel (tap a row in the Final Zone Log table to pick one first). Useful when you just want one test's breakdown on paper without the full history.

🖨 All-Time Daily Qs Report

A button below the Daily Qs table (in the Daily Qs tab) opens a printable report covering every question you've ever logged — totals per subject, total time logged, average pace, and the full history grouped day-by-day: each date gets its own heading, subtotal, and mini-table, instead of one long table mixing every day together.

📚 Study Hours

Below the Daily Qs table, a small box to log today's total self-study hours per subject (Physics/Chemistry/Maths). Saving again today overwrites today's entry rather than duplicating it. The Weekly Report shows your average daily self-study hours (per subject and total) across the last 7 days, based on however many of those days you actually logged.

🧩 Custom Test Log

In the Battle Log tab, below the Mock Score Trend chart. Unlike the two built-in logs, you name the category yourself (PYQ Paper, Speed Drill, whatever) — for any test that doesn't cleanly fit "chapter test" or "full mock."

✎ Editable Entries

Every test log — Chapter Skirmish, Final Zone, Advanced (both), Daily Qs, and Custom — now has an edit (✎) button next to delete on each row. It fills the form with that entry's values and the Add button switches to "✓ Update Entry"; a "Cancel Edit" link appears next to it if you want to back out without saving changes.

🎨 Accent Theme

Data tab → pick from 5 palettes (Inferno Gold, Crimson Elite, Emerald Ops, Cyber Violet, and Minimal Mono). The first four only change the gold/highlight accent — Physics/Chemistry/Maths keep their fixed colors everywhere so the subject color-coding still makes sense. Minimal Mono is different: it strips the app down to black, white, and gray, with one deliberate exception — the HUD, hamburger button, buttons, and gold-tier chapter cards carry a slow-cycling RGB neon glow around their border, so it's not flat monochrome, just color used sparingly as an accent instead of everywhere. Your choice is remembered either way.

🔔 Streak Reminders

Data tab toggle — asks for notification permission, then checks in the evening (8pm+) whether you've logged anything today. If not, it fires a local reminder. Since this app has no server, this only works while the app/browser is actually open and running in the background — it's not a guaranteed always-on push notification like WhatsApp or Instagram use.

🚀 Splash Screen

A brief themed loading screen shows on launch while your saved data loads, instead of a blank flash of white/black.

📝 Daily Qs Tab

Log your day-to-day practice: pick a subject, difficulty level, how many questions you attempted, and which exam it was for (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or a custom "Other" exam). Every entry feeds the subject totals shown on the Story panel and tab badges, and counts toward your daily streak.

⚛ 🧪 ➗ Physics / Chemistry / Maths Tabs

Each chapter has a checklist (Reference Book, NCERT Theory, PYQs by year range, Notes, Lecture). Ticking items updates that chapter's %, the subject summary, and your overall XP.

🌌 JEE Advanced Tab

A separate, tougher checklist layer for chapters you're pushing to Advanced level, with its own counters and its own Weak Spot Radar (Anomaly Scan) — kept independent from the Mains-only Danger Zone.

🎯 Battle Log Tab

  • Danger Zone The most critical Mains chapters right now — badly scoring or fully checklist-complete but never tested. Only these entries get a "try a 50-question test" suggestion.
  • Chapter Skirmish Log Log per-chapter test results to track topic-level performance.
  • Final Zone Log Log full-length mock test results.
  • Mock Score Trend A line chart of your mock % over time — log at least 2 dated mocks to see it.

🏆 Booyah Badges Tab

Achievements you unlock through consistent progress, plus the Weak Spot Radar — chapters dragging your average down across both checklist and test performance.

🌐 Leaderboard Tab

Log in (or continue as guest) via the account bar at the top of the app to join the live, online leaderboard — everyone currently logged in is ranked by League (weekly XP tier) first, plus separate tables for average mock score and subject-wise syllabus completion. Your actual tracker data (checklists, tests, daily questions, coaching log) always stays local on your device — only your name, league, and score summary are shared.
